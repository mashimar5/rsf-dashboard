Privacy

RSF Dashboard · last updated 7 September 2026

RSF Dashboard is a personal project that shows how busy the UC Berkeley RSF weight rooms are. It is operated by one person and is not affiliated with UC Berkeley or with Density, whose sensors provide the occupancy figures.

What it collects without signing in

Nothing about you. The dashboard records occupancy counts for the weight rooms — how many people are present, roughly every five minutes. These are aggregate numbers from a doorway sensor and identify nobody. No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no tracking.

What it collects if you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional and exists only to suggest workout times around your schedule.

  • Free/busy times. The app requests the calendar.freebusy scope, which returns only the start and end times of blocks when you are busy. It does not receive event titles, descriptions, locations, attendees, or any other event content — Google does not send them under this scope.
  • Your email address, used solely to check that the account signing in is the one permitted to.

What is stored

  • Free/busy times are never stored. They are requested when a page loads, used to filter suggested times, and discarded.
  • A Google refresh token, encrypted at rest, so you do not have to sign in on every visit.
  • Your email address, alongside that token.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. Data is not sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party. There are no advertisers, analytics providers, or trackers. The only outbound requests are to Google (for your free/busy times), to Density (for occupancy counts), and to UC Berkeley Recreation & Wellbeing (for opening hours).

Removing your data

Signing out with the "forget me" option deletes the stored refresh token and email. You can also revoke access at any time from Google Account permissions, which immediately invalidates the token regardless of anything stored here.

Where it runs

On a single server hosted by Fly.io in California. The source code is public at github.com/mashimar5/rsf-dashboard, so every claim on this page can be checked against it.
